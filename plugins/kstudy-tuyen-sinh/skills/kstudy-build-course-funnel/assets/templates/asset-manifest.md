## Instructor image requirements

Each instructor referenced by `course_explanation.instructors` needs one image asset row with real owner/source, usage rights or consent, approved status, display dimensions, descriptive alt text and a non-deceptive fallback. Do not substitute stock portraits or AI-generated people for a real instructor.
