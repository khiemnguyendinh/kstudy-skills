# Kstudy Academy Brand Guidelines

## Brand Core

- Brand: Kstudy Academy / Hoc vien Kstudy.
- Tagline: Dao tao AI & Automation Marketing.
- Website: `www.kstudy.edu.vn`.
- Logo sub-line: `Study by case studies`.
- Positioning: Vietnamese training institute for practical AI, automation, digital marketing, growth, content, analytics, and career outcomes.
- Audience: learners, working professionals, founders, managers, marketers, and non-technical operators who want practical capability.

## Voice

- Vietnamese-first. Keep English terms that are normal in the field: AI, automation, marketing, tool, growth, CV, portfolio, workflow, funnel, KPI, ROI.
- Tone: confident, practical, encouraging, modern, technical but easy to understand.
- Address the learner as `ban`. Refer to Kstudy as `Kstudy` or `chung toi`.
- Use outcome-driven copy: what learners can build, apply, show in a CV/portfolio, or use at work.
- Avoid academic, brochure-like, or generic agency wording.
- Avoid emoji in formal brand assets.

## Copy Rules

- Headlines: Vietnamese sentence case with correct diacritics. Short English taglines can use Title Case.
- Eyebrows/tags: uppercase, short, wide tracking. Examples: `LO TRINH 2026`, `AI MARKETING`, `CASE STUDY`, `THUC CHIEN`.
- CTAs: `Dang ky tu van`, `Xem lo trinh`, `Bat dau ngay`, `Nhan lich khai giang`.
- Do not invent numbers. Only use proof points when supplied by the user or source material.

## Visual Personality

- Modern, technical, optimistic, outcome-oriented.
- Foundation: navy trust + bright blue action + yellow highlight.
- Motif: connected node-K icon, grid/dot textures, blended gradient circles, real learner/instructor photography.
- Composition: clear hierarchy, generous spacing, strong contrast, one major focal point per screen/slide.

## Logo Rules

- Use `kstudy-logo-full.png` on light backgrounds.
- Use `kstudy-logo-white.png` on navy/dark/gradient backgrounds.
- Use the icon alone only when space is tight or as a brand mark, not as a generic UI icon.
- Keep clear space around logo. Do not stretch, recolor, outline, or place it over noisy images.

## Imagery

- Prefer real Kstudy photos from `assets/photos/` or user-supplied authentic classroom/team photos. Read `assets/photos/manifest.md` before selecting a local photo.
- Favor people learning, instructors teaching, laptop work, workshops, portfolio/job outcomes, and marketing/AI workflows.
- Grade images slightly cool/navy-blue when needed.
- Use `references/visual-sourcing.md` when deciding between real photos, diagrams, charts, generated images, Canva, or internet-sourced visuals.
- Avoid generic stock, random AI robots, abstract business handshakes, and purely decorative tech wallpaper.
- Do not present generated, stock, or internet people/classes/events as real Kstudy evidence.

## Legacy Note

An older Kstudy design note used navy/orange/Montserrat/Open Sans. Treat that as legacy for old course decks. New work should use the Claude design system packaged in this skill unless the user explicitly asks for the older style.
